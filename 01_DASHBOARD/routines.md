# 🔄 Operating Routines & Rhythms

> **Habits are algorithms for humans. Run them with your AI.**

---

## ☀️ The Morning Kickoff (3 Minutes)
Prompt to give your AI:
> *"Review `01_DASHBOARD/current.md` and `10_SECRETARIAT/daily-brief.md`. What are my 3 non-negotiable wins today, and what is the single hardest blocker I need to kill first?"*

1. Verify P0 task.
2. Clear any incoming notes in `00_INBOX/`.
3. Lock in the deep work block.

---

## 🌙 The Evening Shutdown (2 Minutes)
Prompt to give your AI:
> *"Log what got done today into `01_DASHBOARD/current.md`. Clear completed tasks and queue the exact first step for tomorrow morning."*

1. Mark done tasks.
2. Commit and sync Git changes if applicable.
3. Close all open browser tabs.

---

## 📅 The Sunday Sprint Reset (10 Minutes)
1. Review `03_PROJECTS/` active status.
2. Triage everything in `00_INBOX/`.
3. Pick next week's single theme and update `01_DASHBOARD/current.md`.
