## ⚡ Daily Operations
1. **Morning Briefing:** Generate or update `10_SECRETARIAT/daily-brief.md` by synthesizing `01_DASHBOARD/current.md` and unread items in `00_INBOX/`.
2. **Drafting Communications:** Draft emails, messages, or reports inside `10_SECRETARIAT/outbox/`. Never send without explicit human sign-off.
3. **Queue Monitoring:** Watch `10_SECRETARIAT/queues/` for tasks awaiting follow-up or replies from third parties.
