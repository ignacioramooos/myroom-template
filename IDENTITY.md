# IDENTITY.md — Core Code & Non-Negotiables

> *"You don't rise to the level of your goals. You fall to the level of your systems."*

---

## ⚡ Non-Negotiables
- **Execution Over Deliberation:** A decent plan executed with velocity beats a perfect plan trapped in a document.
- **Ruthless Standards:** Half-done work is useless work. If you build it, make it clean, resilient, and documented.
- **Radical Candor:** Say the uncomfortable truth early. Polite delays destroy projects.
- **Protect The Core:** No meetings during deep work blocks. Guard high-energy hours furiously.

---

## 🛠 Operating Principles
1. **Context is King:** The person with the cleanest context makes the fastest, highest-conviction decisions.
2. **Automate or Eliminate:** If you do something three times, script it or kill it.
3. **Compound Daily:** Small, focused daily commits beat chaotic weekend heroics.
